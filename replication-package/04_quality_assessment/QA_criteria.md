# Quality Assessment Checklist

## Source

Section 2.6 of the manuscript.

## Scoring

Each question is scored as:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1.0 | Yes |
| 0.5 | Partially |
| 0.0 | No |

**Maximum:** 5.0 points  
**Cut-off:** ≥ 3.0 (studies below this threshold were excluded from the primary synthesis)

## Questions

**Q1.** Does the study propose or evaluate an AI-based technique (ML, DL, GNN, or LLM) for smart contract security analysis?

**Q2.** Is the dataset or benchmark used clearly described and, ideally, publicly accessible?

**Q3.** Does the study report quantitative detection metrics (e.g., precision, recall, F1-score, accuracy)?

**Q4.** Is the experimental methodology — baselines, evaluation protocol, and setup — described in enough detail to be reproducible?

**Q5.** Does the study present and discuss its results, including limitations?

## Application

- All 111 reports assessed for eligibility received a quality score.
- 93 studies scored ≥ 3.0 and were included.
- 5 studies were excluded specifically for scoring below the cut-off.
- The remaining 13 exclusions at the eligibility stage were for other reasons (9 not addressing AI for smart-contract security, 4 focused on non-security topics).
